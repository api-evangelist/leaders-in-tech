# Leaders In Tech

> Leaders In Tech (LIT) is a San Francisco leadership accelerator for startup founders and technology
> executives, founded in 2015 (Y Combinator Summer 2015). It runs cohort-based fellowship programs
> built on T-groups — small-group experiential learning — for founders, early-stage founders and
> senior executives, plus corporate workshops and retreats. Leaders In Tech publishes no public API,
> developer portal or SDK; this file describes the organization, not a developer interface.

## Programs

- [Founder Fellows Program](https://www.leadersintech.org/founder-fellows-program-overview): 10 months, for founders and CEOs at Series A through pre-IPO (>$5M raised).
- [Grove Fellows Program](https://www.leadersintech.org/grove-fellows-program): 6 months, for pre-seed and seed stage founders.
- [Executive Program](https://www.leadersintech.org/executive-program): 6 months, for senior leaders and executives.
- [Corporate Workshops](https://www.leadersintech.org/corporate-workshops): Workshops delivered to companies.
- [Retreats](https://www.leadersintech.org/retreats): In-person retreat offerings.
